EEG datasets

Where open EEG data lives, and how much of it there is

This page catalogs open EEG data: the places it is published, and the datasets themselves with their rough size. It is a map for finding data, not a benchmark. Every number comes from the dataset’s own page or paper, and a blank means the source doesn’t report it.

Benchmark libraries cover one paradigm family each: 200 of MOABB’s 206 datasets, for example, are BCI paradigms (motor imagery, ERP, SSVEP and c-VEP), and it loads none of the large clinical or sleep corpora. Labs also tend to publish to one repository each, so no single source sees everything. The tables below span EEG repositories, general-purpose repositories, clinical corpora and lab websites.

The catalog is two CSV files in the repo, catalog/sources.csv and catalog/datasets.csv, so you can load, filter or correct it. Each row there also records the license, the reference, the page its numbers came from, and notes on how they were counted, including which numbers are approximate. Numbers were gathered on 3 November 2026.

Access:

  • open: download directly, no account
  • registration: free account or login
  • DUA: signed data use agreement or credentialed access
  • request: apply to the lab or authors

Trials counts trials across all subjects and sessions; hours is total recording time. Dataset tables are sorted by number of subjects, and each name links to the dataset’s landing page.

Where EEG data lives

Three kinds of places publish EEG data. Counts are as each source reported them on 3 November 2026.

General-purpose repositories

Repositories that hold any kind of data and don’t tag EEG reliably. The count is search hits for “EEG”, which overstates how many EEG datasets they hold: Zenodo’s hits even include Germany’s renewable-energy law, which is also abbreviated EEG. Much lab-published BCI data lives here all the same.

Source Search hits Access Notes
Figshare 7,562 open items of type dataset returned by the API search (search_for=EEG, item_type=3), which includes journal supplementary files. Hosts 17 MOABB datasets, largely Chinese groups (e.g., Tsinghua eldBETA) plus Greek MAMEM and Mexican BCI data.
OSF (Open Science Framework) 2,689 open public OSF projects matching ‘EEG’ in the SHARE index (cardSearchText=EEG, resourceType=Project; 3,349 with components), many holding code or preregistrations rather than data. Favoured by psychology/ERP labs (ERP CORE) and Korea University’s mobile and 2020-competition BCI sets in MOABB.
Kaggle datasets 1,558 registration unique results of ‘kaggle datasets list -s EEG’ until pages stopped returning items. Mostly user re-uploads of public datasets (e.g., an OpenNeuro ds002778 copy, SAM40), so check provenance and licence.
Zenodo 1,531 mixed hits for q=EEG with type=dataset; the query also catches Germany’s renewable-energy law (EEG), and ‘electroencephalography OR electroencephalogram’ gives 421. Largest home of MOABB BCI data (54 of 206), led by French labs such as GIPSA-lab Grenoble (Brain Invaders) and Inria.
Hugging Face Hub 1,001 mixed datasets whose name matches ‘eeg’, of which 738 are EEGDash mirrors of NEMAR/OpenNeuro data; a further 13 datasets sit under the braindecode org. The remaining ~260 are user uploads of mixed provenance and licence.
Harvard Dataverse 535 mixed datasets returned by the Search API for q=EEG, type=dataset. 10 MOABB BCI datasets live here, 7 from Chinese groups (Tianjin University, CAS, World Robot Contest) and 2 from Japan.
Mendeley Data 413 open results attributed to Mendeley Data for query=EEG on its search page (419 across all indexed sources). Elsevier’s repository; its 5 MOABB datasets come from Mexico, Ecuador, Indonesia and Turkey.
NIMH Data Archive (NDA) 278 DUA collections matching ‘EEG’ in NDA’s search API (80 match ‘electroencephalography’). EEG from NIH-funded mental-health studies (autism, psychosis, depression, adolescent sleep); access needs an institutionally signed Data Use Certification.
IEEE DataPort 213 mixed results for site search ‘EEG’: 181 standard datasets (subscription), 24 open access, 8 competitions. Mostly single-lab engineering datasets, often from consumer headsets (e.g., the Emotiv-based STEW workload set).
Dryad 157 open hits for q=EEG in the Dryad search API (60 for ‘electroencephalography’). Journal-linked CC0 deposits, human and animal; few large curated EEG collections.
G-Node GIN 45 open public repositories matching ‘EEG’ from the GIN API, including forks and DOI copies. Small German/Swiss footprint (MPIB Berlin, USZ Zurich scalp EEG + iEEG, mouse hdEEG).
EBRAINS Knowledge Graph 42 mixed datasets matching ‘EEG’ (21 tagged electroencephalography; 13 controlled access, 12 free, 9 embargoed, 8 restricted). European Human Brain Project labs, e.g., TMS-EEG perturbational complexity and sEEG/hd-EEG stimulation studies.
Synapse (Sage Bionetworks) 25 mixed public projects matching ‘EEG’ in the Synapse search API, several unrelated (e.g., mPower). Hosts TD-BRAIN (Brainclinics), BrainLat, HarMNqEEG multinational cross-spectra and FACED; access terms vary by project.
DANDI Archive 23 open dandisets matching ‘EEG’; mostly animal and intracranial (iEEG/sEEG, single-unit) recordings. DANDI is the BRAIN Initiative’s NWB archive, with little human scalp EEG.
GigaDB 8 open 8 EEG datasets among the 11 hits for ‘EEG’ in the GigaDB search API. Data notes for GigaScience; all 4 MOABB datasets here are Korean (Korea University OpenBMI Lee2019, GIST Cho2017).
UCI Machine Learning Repository 4 open 4 datasets match ‘EEG’ in its API (EEG Database, EEG Eye State, EEG SSVEP signals, BEED epilepsy). Small, old ML-benchmark staples.
